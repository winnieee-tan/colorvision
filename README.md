## 🎯 The Problem

Over **300 million people** worldwide live with Color Vision Deficiency (affecting 1 in 12 men and 1 in 200 women):
* **Protanopia**: Reduced sensitivity to red light, causing confusion between reds, greens, and browns.
* **Tritanopia**: Inability to distinguish blue and yellow shades.

In everyday life, this creates significant accessibility barriers:
* Critical UI status indicators (error vs. success states).
* Traffic signals, warning markers, and public transit maps.
* Complex data visualizations, financial charts, and medical imaging.

Standard color-blind filters often wash out or unnaturally tint the entire image, destroying natural lighting and contextual contrast.

---

## 💡 The Solution: ColorVision

**ColorVision** approaches color correction as a **data-driven transformation pipeline**:

1. **Context-Preserving Transformation**: Rather than applying a blanket tint, ColorVision analyzes the chromatic distribution of the scene and selectively remaps confusable color vectors while maintaining luminance balance.
2. **Personalized Profiles**: Supports custom transformation matrices calibrated specifically for **Protanopia** and **Tritanopia**.
3. **Real-Time Video Ready**: Architected to process streaming video frames with minimal computational overhead.

---

## 🚀 Future Vision: AR Smart Glasses

The long-term vision of ColorVision is ambient assistive technology:
* **AR Integration**: Deploying the color transformation pipeline onto **augmented reality smart glasses** (e.g., optical see-through displays).
* **Live Camera Stream Correction**: Wearers looking at traffic lights, charts, or signage receive subtle, real-time edge and contrast enhancements directly in their field of vision without feeling visually overwhelmed.
